10-minute triage

Rule: one FAIL in any red box = hard no. Thank the agent, photograph the reason, move on. Cosmetic damage is ignored entirely. Kit: bright torch, flat screwdriver or awl, small spirit level or a marble, phone.

  1. From the road (2 min) — sight the ridge line end-to-end; walk the exterior foundation; look at where downpipes discharge.
  2. Genkan (1 min) — smell test as you enter; try every sliding door and the front door.
  3. Wet areas (3 min) — bath, toilet, kitchen: floor bounce, ceiling above, base of walls.
  4. Under the floor (3 min) — hatch is usually in the kitchen or wash-room floor (床下点検口). Torch + screwdriver on the sill (土台) and post bases.
  5. Roof space if quick (1 min) — push up a closet ceiling panel (押入れ) with the torch: daylight, stains, sag.
  6. Verdict + photos — log it in the card before getting in the car.
